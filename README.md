# blood-pressure-tracker
Personal Blood Pressure Tracker PWA

BP Tracker

A simple, private blood pressure tracking app.

A lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA) for recording daily blood pressure readings, monitoring weekly averages, and keeping a personal health history.

Designed to be minimal, easy to use, and accessible from your phone or desktop.

Open BP Tracker · Buy me a coffee ☕

Important

BP Tracker is a personal health logging tool, not a medical device.

It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice. Always discuss concerning readings with a qualified healthcare professional.

Your readings are stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. They are not uploaded to GitHub or a remote database.

Keep backups of your readings. Browser storage can be lost if website data is cleared or the browser is removed.

Highlights
Simple daily input — Record systolic, diastolic, and pulse readings.
Time adjustment — Move the reading time forward or backward by one hour, or select a custom time.
Custom dates — Record readings from previous days.
Weekly averages — Automatically calculate systolic and diastolic averages over the last seven calendar days.
Reading history — View recent measurements, including dates, times, and notes.
Multiple readings — Record multiple measurements throughout the day.
Offline support — Designed to work without an internet connection after the app has been loaded and cached.
Installable PWA — Add it to your phone's home screen.
Minimalist design — Soft green and white interface, designed for comfortable daily use.

Installation

BP Tracker runs directly in your browser. No app store or installation package is required.

On Android
Open BP Tracker in Chrome.
Open the browser menu.
Select Install app or Add to Home screen.
Open BP Tracker from your home screen.
On iPhone
Open the app in Safari.
Tap the Share button.
Select Add to Home Screen.
Tap Add.
Offline usage

Open the app while connected to the internet at least once to allow its files to be cached.

Afterward, the app is designed to remain accessible offline.

Privacy and data storage

BP Tracker uses the browser's built-in IndexedDB database.

No user account required.
No cloud database.
No external health-data service.
No automatic upload of measurements.
Data stays within the browser's local website storage.

Your readings are associated with the browser and website origin on your device.

Clearing browser storage may permanently remove your readings. Backup and restore functionality is planned.
